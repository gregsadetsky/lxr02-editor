LXR-02 kit editor

Micro Manual
1) connect your LXR-02 over MIDI (you must use Chrome)
2) choose a kit — it should load on your LXR-02
3) you can type K T G or any letter below to trigger drums — BUT FIRST make sure that the note and midi channel are correct
3.1) to check the midi channel+note: go to 'voice' mode, pick a drum, press 'mix', and scroll right until you see "ch" and "nte"
