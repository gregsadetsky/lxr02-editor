Micro Manual
1) connect your LXR-02 over MIDI (you must use Chrome)
2) play around or choose a kit (it should load on your LXR-02)
3) you can type K T G or any letter below to trigger drums — BUT FIRST make sure that the notes and midi channels are correct
3.1) check the midi channel+note: press 'voice', pick a drum, press 'mix', and scroll right until you see "ch" and "nte"
